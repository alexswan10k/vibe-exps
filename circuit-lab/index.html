Circuit DSL — v2

Describe what connects to what, one relation per line. No coordinates — layout is automatic, like mermaid.

# --- links: single dash is a wire, double dash carries a part ---
A - B
A -- R1 10k -- B
VCC -- R1 10k -- OUT -- R2 10k -- GND

# --- hops can mix freely ---
A - B -- R1 10k -- C - D

# --- explicit form (ID TYPE VALUE NETS...) ---
V1 V 9V VCC GND
Q1 QN N1 N2 GND      # transistor: collector base emitter
U1 OPAMP GND N1 OUT  # op-amp: IN+ IN- OUT
G1 GND N1            # ground symbol on N1

# --- plain copper ---
wire N1 N2

Nets named VCC 5V 9V V+ pin to the top rail, GND 0V to the bottom rail — use them to simplify: anything returning to ground just says GND.

Types: R C L D LED V I BAT SW FUSE W SPK M (2-pin) · QN QP · OPAMP · GND VCC PORT. In links the type is inferred from the refdes (R C L D V I Q U …); D1 LED forces an LED.

Simulation: every render runs a DC operating point (MNA + Newton–Raphson) and, when the circuit has C, L, or an AC source, a transient — shown as voltage badges on the schematic, tables below, and a scope plot. V1 V 12VAC AC1 AC2 is a 50 Hz mains sine (RMS); V1 V SIN(5 1000) IN GND is a 5 V-peak 1 kHz sine. Stimulus step switches all DC sources on at t=0 so you can watch RC charge and LC ring.

Values are free text — 10k 100n 2N2222 LM358 9V. Unknown nets are created on demand.
