Under the hood

The light is bent, not faked

Every pixel fires a photon from the camera and integrates its path through the curved spacetime around a non-rotating black hole. Nothing here is a sprite or a lens distortion — the shadow, the arcs over the top, and the bright rim are all consequences of the same equation.

d²u/dφ² + u = (3/2) · rs · u²

The null geodesic equation in the Schwarzschild metric, with u = 1/r. In Cartesian form the shader integrates a = −(3/2) · h² · r / |r|⁵, where h = |r × v| is conserved.

  • Event horizon at r = rs. Anything crossing it is gone; the shader simply stops the ray.
  • Photon sphere at 1.5 rs. Light can orbit here, which is why the shadow has a radius of about 2.6 rs — larger than the horizon itself.
  • ISCO at 3 rs. The disk starts here; closer in, stable orbits don't exist.
  • Doppler beaming: the side of the disk moving toward you is brighter and bluer. Brightness scales roughly with g³, and the observed temperature with g.
  • Gravitational redshift: light climbing out of the well loses energy, dimming and reddening the far side.

Rendered in a single WebGL2 fragment shader with adaptive resolution, a multi-pass bloom, and ACES tonemapping. ~200 integration steps per pixel. Units: G = c = rs = 1.
