🧱 how to survive

  1. Hold LMB on a tree → logs. Zombies come at night — hurry.
  2. E → recipe book: search, filter, Craft 1-click (shift = all). ▦ fills the grid the old way.
  3. Craft a pickaxe (stone), axe (logs), shovel (dirt), + sword. Place the table (RMB) for 3×3.
  4. Mine stone → coal → iron → gold → diamond (deeper = richer). F smelts: iron, gold, pork, beef, chicken, sand → glass. Caves wind below — stair-hole entrances drop in, ores show on the walls, bring torches.
  5. Bed (RMB or F): sets your spawn. Ladders: Space climbs. Cows → beef, chickens → drumsticks.
  6. Place torches (RMB) before dark. Click animals/zombies to attack. TNT: place, aim, F to light — obsidian survives blasts.
  7. 🤝 Co-op: TAB shows players + a kill/death scoreboard · the kill feed sits under the minimap · both players mining the same block mine at 2× speed · chests, engines, pipes and quarries are shared · Q pings a block for your partner (15s marker)
  8. G eats pork · T chat · N minimap (green spawn · orange home · purple bed) · shift sprint · H this panel · P toggle shadows
  9. R cast fishing rod (needs rod: sticks + string, wait for a bite, then F the furnace to cook it) · F on a 🐺 wolf to tame — it fights hostiles beside you · RMB on a 🧑‍🌾 villager to trade · holding a torch lights your way · beware ☠️ lava lakes deep down
  10. /sethome /home fast-travel · /rain summon storms (storms shield undead from sun!) · beware the 👹 ogre
  11. wheel / 1-9 cycle hotbar · double-click food in inventory to eat · ESC closes inventory/panels

🔧 BuildCraft
chest (F/RMB, 27 slots) · engine (F/RMB panel → coal/oil/lava, auto-sucks from adjacent chests/tanks) · pipes move items chest→chest while an engine burns · quarry auto-mines a 9×9 under itself beside a fueled engine. Oil ore glows in deserts.
🧪 Fluids
bucket (3 iron) scoops water/lava with F · tank holds 16 buckets · pump taps adjacent water/lava while powered · fluid pipes balance tanks. A lava bucket burns an engine for 120s.
⚔️ Weapons
bow + arrows (X to shoot) · warhammer (12 dmg launcher) · dagger · firebrand. C stocks a creative block kit.
🚂 Vehicles
boat (RMB on water) sails fast — WASD + shift · minecart + steam locomotive need rails (RMB), W/S throttle, /fuel nearby, locomotive pulls connected carts. F hops in/out.
🏘 Villages & trading
3–5 villages with houses, well and farm. RMB a villager for trade offers, pay with emeralds from emerald ore deep down.
🏚 Dungeons & 👹 bosses
Cobble rooms at y9–21 with furnaces and a skeleton guardian that respawns. The rare nightly ogre (90 HP) drops diamonds.
🐺 Tames & 🎣 fishing
F on a wolf tames it (it fights for you) · R casts a rod (sticks + string), cook the catch.
🌀 Travel
G throws an ender pearl (12 blocks) · 🧭 compass points at home/spawn · Q pings a block for your partner (15s marker).
